Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who uses it

Post Scheduler is a private tool used only by its owner to schedule uploads to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. No one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos to the chosen YouTube channel, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, change their privacy setting, and read the channel's name and handle to confirm it is the right channel. For Instagram, it receives permission to publish Reels to the owner's accounts and read their usernames.

What it stores and where

Sign-in tokens, the post queue and a log of posts are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs. The tool collects no data about viewers or anyone else, uses no analytics, and shares nothing with third parties.

Google user data

Post Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, and in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Deleting the tool's local files removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
