What it is

Post Scheduler is a private tool its owner runs on their own computer to schedule videos to their own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and TikTok accounts. It is not sold or offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

How it posts

Every post is approved by the owner before it goes out. On TikTok, the tool only sends a video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft; the owner reviews it, writes the caption, chooses who can see it and posts it from the TikTok app.

Use of the tool is subject to the terms and community guidelines of each platform it posts to, including TikTok's Terms of Service, YouTube's Terms of Service and Instagram's Terms of Use. The owner is responsible for the content they post.
